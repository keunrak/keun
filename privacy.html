Privacy Policy

Keun Workspace is a personal app used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive data only to read, draft, schedule and send the owner's email and to read and manage the owner's calendar and files.

Data is processed on the owner's own computer and by AI assistants the owner chooses to use. It is not sold, shared with other parties, or used for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
